Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

At GMUN, we envision a vibrant community of students, faculties, and individuals united by a shared passion for fostering global citizenship, critical thinking, and effective communication. Our mission is to provide a platform for young minds to engage, debate, and collaborate on pressing global issues, empowering them to become empathetic, informed, and active participants in shaping a better world.

We are committed to building a vocally diverse community in which various perspectives are valued, respected, and nurtured — hosting events, initiatives, and activities with school students, college students, and faculties from diverse backgrounds, in a safe, supportive, and stimulating environment that allows individuals to express themselves freely, challenge their assumptions, and grow as global citizens.

We know that personal prejudices and biases dilute our ability to relate to people, empathize with other viewpoints, and make logical decisions. We are therefore committed to helping people overcome such biases, respecting facts and statistics, and developing a multi-faceted view of things through our workshops and events.

As a dynamic and vibrant community, we endeavor to positively contribute to our communities, our country, and global society — building, together, a more equitable and sustainable future for all.

About

About GMUN

GMUN is one of the leading clubs at GITAM University, functioning under the Directorate of Student Life. What started as a Student Interest Group has grown into a recognized club that gives students a real platform to debate, negotiate, and think through the issues shaping the world today.

We believe that staying informed isn't enough on its own. GMUN exists to help students move from simply reading the news to actively engaging with it, through Model UN-style debates, crisis simulations, policy discussions, and peer-led conversations that bring global issues into everyday campus life.

Our Vision & Mission

We envision a community of students, faculty, and individuals brought together by a shared interest in global citizenship, critical thinking, and clear communication. Our mission is to give young minds a space to engage, debate, and work together on the world's pressing issues, helping them grow into empathetic, informed, and active citizens.

What We Aim to Do

  • Build confidence and public speaking skills — sharpening debate and negotiation skills.
  • Bring global affairs into everyday campus life — making real discussion a normal part of student life.
  • Sharpen critical thinking — pushing students to see problems from more than one side.

Why GMUN Matters

  • Learn by doing — turning classroom theory into hands-on experience.
  • Think across subjects — drawing on international relations and political science.
  • Build a wider network — connecting members across institutions.
  • Prepare for what's next — public speaking, negotiation, and diplomacy skills for any career.
  • Add to campus life — through events, workshops, and guest lectures.

About GITAM Deemed to be University

Founded in 1980, GITAM is a multidisciplinary deemed-to-be university inspired by the ideals of Mahatma Gandhi. Today, it brings together education, research, innovation, and holistic development across its campuses in Visakhapatnam, Bengaluru, and Hyderabad.

GITAM's educational philosophy focuses on creating a knowledge-driven environment where students are encouraged to think independently, pursue meaningful ideas, and contribute to society.

GITAM Visakhapatnam

The Visakhapatnam campus serves as GITAM's main hub and is situated between the Eastern Ghats and the Bay of Bengal, giving the campus a distinctive natural setting. It brings together multiple schools and disciplines, including management, humanities and social sciences, science, computer science, engineering, architecture, pharmacy, and law.

The campus combines academic spaces with facilities for student life, sports, dining, and accommodation, creating an environment designed around both learning and overall student development.

About Visakhapatnam

Where the coast meets the city

Visakhapatnam, popularly known as Vizag, is a coastal city on the eastern shore of India, nestled between the Bay of Bengal and the Eastern Ghats. Known for its beaches, hills, maritime heritage, and vibrant culture, the city offers a distinctive setting for students and visitors from across the country.

As one of Andhra Pradesh's major cities and an important port and industrial centre, Visakhapatnam combines a growing modern identity with a rich historical and cultural heritage. It is also home to the Eastern Naval Command of the Indian Navy.

For GMUN delegates, Visakhapatnam offers more than just a conference destination — its coastline, heritage, food, and surrounding landscapes provide plenty to explore beyond committee rooms.

What is MUN?

An educational simulation of the real UN

Model United Nations, or MUN, is an educational simulation of the real United Nations. It gives students a hands-on way to learn how the UN works — not from a textbook, but by stepping into the role of a diplomat.

At a MUN conference, each student takes on the role of a "delegate" representing a country, or sometimes an organization. Delegates research their assigned country's actual position on a real global issue, then debate, negotiate, and try to reach agreement with other delegates, much like real UN diplomats do. Delegates argue for their assigned country's stance, not their own personal opinions — part of what makes MUN such a valuable exercise in seeing an issue from more than one side.

MUN is not a small or recent trend. It began not long after the UN itself was founded, and today hundreds of thousands of students, from primary school through university, take part in MUN conferences around the world every year. Through General Assembly Resolution 77/336, the UN formally acknowledged Model UN's role in engaging young people with its work and building support for international cooperation.

Why Participate in MUN?

  • Build real skills, not just theory — MUN sharpens public speaking, research, and writing skills through actual practice.
  • Get a head start on global affairs — for many students, MUN is their first real introduction to issues like peace and security, human rights, and sustainable development.
  • Learn to negotiate and find common ground — MUN conferences often work toward consensus rather than a simple vote.
  • Step into someone else's shoes — representing a country that isn't your own builds empathy and a broader worldview.
  • Open doors for the future — many of today's leaders in law, government, business, and the arts took part in Model UN as students.

FAQ's

01What is GMUN 5.0?
GMUN 5.0 is the fifth edition of the GITAM Model United Nations, bringing together students to engage in diplomacy, debate, negotiation, and discussions on contemporary global and national issues.
02Who can participate in GMUN 5.0?
GMUN 5.0 is open to students who meet the eligibility criteria specified by the conference. Participants from schools and colleges may register, subject to the rules of the respective committees.
03Do I need prior MUN experience?
No. Prior MUN experience is not mandatory. Both first-time delegates and experienced MUN participants are welcome.
04Where will GMUN 5.0 be held?
GMUN 5.0 will be held at GITAM Deemed to be University, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh.
05When will GMUN 5.0 take place?
The conference will be held on 24th & 25th of October 2026. Please check the official conference schedule for timings and session details.
06What committees are available?
GMUN 5.0 will feature multiple committees with different agendas and formats. Explore the Committees section to view available committees, agendas, and the Executive Board.
07Can I choose my committee?
Yes. Participants can indicate their preferred committee during registration. Committee allocation is subject to availability and the conference's allocation process.
08Can I choose my portfolio?
You may be able to submit portfolio preferences during registration. However, final portfolio allocation is at the discretion of the Organising team and subject to availability.
09How much does registration cost?
The registration fee for GMUN 5.0 is INR 900. Please refer to the Registration page for the latest fee information.
10What does the registration fee include?
The registration fee includes conference participation and the facilities/services specified by the organizers. Any additional inclusions such as meals, kits, certificates, or accommodation will be mentioned on the Registration page.
11How can I register?
Click Register Now on the website, complete the registration form, select your preferences, and proceed to payment.
12Is payment mandatory to complete registration?
Yes. Registration is considered complete only after the required payment has been successfully processed, unless the Organising team specifically provides an alternative arrangement.
13What payment methods are accepted?
Payments can be made through the payment options provided on the official registration portal.
14What happens after I complete registration?
Once your registration and payment are successfully processed, you will receive a registration confirmation and be added to the delegate WhatsApp group. Further updates will be communicated through official channels.
15I made the payment but didn't receive a confirmation. What should I do?
First, check your email, including spam/junk. If the payment was successful but your registration hasn't been confirmed, contact the GMUN Organising team with your payment/reference details.
16Can I cancel my registration?
Cancellation and refund policies depend on the official GMUN 5.0 registration policy. Please check the Terms & Conditions / Refund Policy before registering.
17Can I transfer my registration to someone else?
Registration transfers are subject to approval from the GMUN Organising team. Please contact the organizers before making any changes.
18When will I receive my portfolio allocation?
Portfolio allocations will be released by the Organising team according to the official GMUN 5.0 timeline. Registered delegates will be notified through official communication channels.
19Where can I find the study guides?
Study guides and other delegate resources will be uploaded to the Committee section of the website as they are released.
20What are the rules and dress code?
Detailed conference rules, Rules of Procedure, dress code, and other guidelines will be updated through the message channels.

Yes. Participants who fulfil the conference requirements will receive a certificate of participation. Additional certificates or awards may be provided according to the conference's award structure.
22Are awards given at GMUN 5.0?
Yes. Delegates may be recognized through committee-specific awards and other categories determined by the Organising team and Executive Board.

Details regarding meals and refreshments will be announced on the conference information page and/or communicated to registered delegates.

Accommodation arrangements, if offered, will be mentioned separately on the official website. Participants should not assume accommodation is included in the registration fee unless explicitly stated.
